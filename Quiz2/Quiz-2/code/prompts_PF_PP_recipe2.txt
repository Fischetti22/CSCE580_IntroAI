Rhubarb Crumble (Vegan)
Source: https://www.instructables.com/Rhubarb-Crumble-Vegan/
Retrieved: 2025-10-07T20:55:59Z

Ingredients:
- Rhubarb - 5 sticks/ 500g /17.5oz/2.5 cups
- Sugar - 125g / 4.4oz / 1/2 cup
- Plain Flour - 250g /8.8oz / 1 cup
- Vegan Butter or Margarine - 100g / 3.5oz / Just under 1/2 a cup

Directions:
1) Remove the outer skin from the rhubarb and chop the sticks into chunks.
2) Place the rhubarb chunks into an oven-proof dish and sprinkle 125 g of sugar over the rhubarb.
3) Add 250 g of plain flour and 100 g of vegan margarine or butter into a blender and blend for about 5 minutes.
4) Pour the crumble over the rhubarb.
5) Cook at 180 C / 350 F for 40 minutes until the crumble is golden brown.
6) Serve warm; optionally with custard or ice cream.
