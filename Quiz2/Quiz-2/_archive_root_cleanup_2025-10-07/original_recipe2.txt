Rhubarb Crumble (Vegan)
Source: https://www.instructables.com/Rhubarb-Crumble-Vegan/
Retrieved: 2025-10-07T20:56:41Z

Ingredients (from page; normalize units as needed):
- Rhubarb stalks, chopped (about 4 cups / ~500 g)
- Granulated sugar (about 1/2 cup / 100 g), divided
- Lemon juice (1–2 tbsp) and zest (optional)
- All-purpose flour (about 1 cup / 125 g)
- Rolled oats (about 1/2 cup / 50 g)
- Brown sugar (about 1/3 cup / 70 g)
- Vegan butter or coconut oil (about 6 tbsp / 85 g), chilled
- Pinch of salt
- Cinnamon (optional)

Instructions (atomic steps):
1) Heat oven to 180°C / 350°F.
2) Wash and chop rhubarb into ~2 cm pieces; pat dry.
3) In a bowl, toss rhubarb with half of the granulated sugar and lemon juice; set aside 10 minutes to macerate.
4) In a separate bowl, make crumble: mix flour, oats, brown sugar, salt, and cinnamon (if using).
5) Cut vegan butter (or coconut oil) into the dry mix until coarse crumbs form.
6) Drain excess liquid from rhubarb (if any); taste and adjust sweetness with remaining granulated sugar.
7) Spread rhubarb evenly in a baking dish.
8) Sprinkle crumble mixture evenly over rhubarb; do not pack down.
9) Bake at 180°C / 350°F until topping is golden and filling is bubbling at edges (about 30–40 min).
10) Cool on rack 10–15 minutes before serving.

Notes:
- Sweetness of rhubarb varies; adjust sugar to taste.
- Serve with vegan ice cream or custard.
