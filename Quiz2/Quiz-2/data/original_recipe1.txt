Blackened Tofu and Vegetables
Source: https://www.instructables.com/Blackened-Tofu-and-Vegetables/
Retrieved: 2025-10-07T20:46:55Z

Supplies / Ingredients:
- Blackening Rub (DIY blend):
  - 4 tbsp Hungarian sweet paprika
  - 2 tbsp onion powder
  - 4 tsp salt (pink Himalayan sea salt noted)
  - 1 tsp ground black pepper
  - 1 tsp ground white pepper
  - 1/2 tsp dried thyme leaves
  - 1/2 tsp dried basil leaves
  - 1/2 tsp dried oregano leaves
- 3 blocks firm tofu (cut to 1/2" thick; pat dry)  OR  eggplant (1 medium) OR potato (1 large) OR spinach (1 box)
- 1.5 tbsp butter
- Fresh basil leaves (handful) for garnish

Instructions (atomic steps):
1) Make blackening rub: measure and mix the listed spices.
2) Melt butter in a large nonstick skillet on low heat.
3) Slice tofu to 1/2" thickness; pat dry with paper towel.
4) Sprinkle blackening rub generously on one side of tofu slices.
5) Increase heat to medium; place tofu spice-side down in skillet.
6) Sprinkle blackening rub on top side of tofu.
7) Cook first side for several minutes; flip and cook the other side for several minutes.
8) Garnish with fresh basil leaves in the pan so they wilt slightly; serve hot.
9) For vegetables: slice eggplant/potato to ~1/2" and blacken using same method; for spinach, wilt in skillet after tofu.

Notes:
- Author mentions using same rub/method for chicken or salmon; not part of vegetarian/vegan variant.
